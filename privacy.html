Quick summary

ZED SCHOLAR helps Zambian secondary learners study with ECZ past papers, marking schemes, quizzes, documents and videos. To do that we need a small amount of information from you: your name, school, grade and how you log in. We use it only to run your account, show your progress and your school leaderboard. We do not sell your data, we do not share your phone number or password with other learners, and we do not read your contacts, messages outside ZED, or your exact location.

1. What we collect

When you create an account

  • Name - your real name, so teachers and friends can find you on the leaderboard.
  • Phone number - 10 digits, if you sign up with phone + password or phone OTP. Used only to log you in.
  • School, grade, province and district - so we can show your school leaderboard and relevant papers.
  • Profile photo - only if you choose to add one. You can change it later in Settings.
  • Account code - like ZED-123456, generated for your account.

When you use the app

  • Quiz attempts, quiz scores, coins and streaks.
  • Which papers and documents you open, for offline access.
  • Feedback / messages you send us from inside the app.
  • Basic device and app info, for example browser type and whether you are offline, to fix bugs.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your contacts, your SMS, your messages outside ZED, or your exact GPS location.
  • We do not collect your Google password or your phone password in plain text for other people to see. Passwords are used only to log you in.
  • We do not collect payment information. The app is free.

2. If you sign in with Google

When you tap Continue with Google, Google asks you for permission to share limited profile information with ZED SCHOLAR. If you agree, Google sends us:

  • Your name as shown on your Google account
  • Your email address
  • Your Google profile photo, if you have one
  • A unique Google user ID, so we can link your Google login to your ZED account

Google does not send us your Google password, and we never ask for it. We store your name, email and photo in our database (Supabase) in the same way we store phone accounts, so you can log in next time with Google and keep the same progress, coins and streak.

You can disconnect Google at any time by logging out in ZED SCHOLAR and removing ZED SCHOLAR from your Google Account > Security > Third-party access. If you disconnect, your ZED account stays, you can still log in with phone + password if you set one in Settings.

Google's handling of your data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows Google's User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google data only to provide and improve sign-in and your account.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and keep your account, and let you log in with phone, OTP or Google.
  • To show your progress, quiz scores, coins and streak.
  • To show your name, school and quiz scores on the school leaderboard. That is how the app works - if you do not want your name on the leaderboard, contact us and we can hide it.
  • To show you the right papers for your grade and school.
  • To fix bugs, keep the app secure, and stop fake accounts cheating the leaderboard.
  • To reply to feedback you send us.

We do not use your data for any other purpose, and we do not use it to show you ads based on your personal profile.

4. Who we share it with

We share only what is needed to run the app, with these service providers:

  • Supabase - our database and login service. Your profile, quiz attempts and documents are stored there. See Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google - if you use Google sign-in, and for Google AdSense ads and reCAPTCHA / security if enabled. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare Pages - hosts this website and delivers the app files.

We do not sell your data. We do not share your phone number, email or password with other learners. Your name, school and quiz scores are visible on the leaderboard to other ZED learners, as described above. If we are required by Zambian law to share data, we will only share the minimum required.

5. Where it is stored and how long we keep it

Your data is stored securely in our database (Supabase) and on your own phone for offline papers and login. We keep your account data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your profile, phone, email and quiz history within 30 days, except for anonymised leaderboard statistics that cannot identify you, and any data we must keep for legal or security reasons.

6. How we protect it

  • Connections to the app use HTTPS.
  • Access to the database is controlled by Supabase Row Level Security and API keys.
  • Passwords are used only for login and are not shown to other users.
  • If you log out on a shared phone, log back in with your own phone + password or Google. Do not stay logged in on a phone that is not yours.

No system is 100% secure. If you think someone has used your account, change your password and contact us immediately.

7. Cookies, analytics and ads

We use local storage on your phone to keep you logged in and to save papers for offline use. The website may use cookies for:

  • Keeping you logged in (Supabase auth session).
  • Remembering your theme and settings.
  • Google AdSense ads, which may use cookies to show relevant ads and measure them. You can control ad personalisation in your Google Ad Settings.

We do not use advertising cookies to build a profile of you from your quiz results.

8. Your controls - edit, clear, delete

  • Edit: Settings > Profile - change your name, school, grade and photo.
  • Clear quiz history: Profile > Clear Quiz History.
  • Log out: Settings > Account > Log out.
  • Delete your account: Send us a message from Settings > Feedback with “Delete my account”, or email chindumbachaka2007@gmail.com from the email or phone on your account. We will confirm and delete within 30 days.
  • Access / correction: Email us and we will send you the data we hold for your account and correct it if it is wrong.

9. Children and learners under 18

ZED SCHOLAR is made for secondary learners in Zambia, many of whom are under 18. We only collect the school information listed above, and we do not collect sensitive information from children. If you are under 18, please ask a parent, guardian or teacher before creating an account. If a parent or guardian asks us to delete a child's account, we will do so. Contact us at the email below.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we collect or use data, we will update the date at the top of this page and show the new policy in the app under Settings > Privacy & Policy. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the new policy.
